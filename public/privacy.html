Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

SciFit is a fitness planning and AI coaching app developed by Aryan Wagh. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Information we collect

  • Account information, such as your email address, when you choose to sign in.
  • Profile and fitness information you provide, including age, sex, height, weight, goals, equipment, limitations, sleep, and soreness.
  • Workout and nutrition logs you enter.
  • Questions, images, and videos you submit for AI-assisted analysis.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as authentication and request records maintained by our service providers.

How we use information

We use your information to provide personalized training and nutrition guidance, save your logs and preferences, analyze content you submit, retrieve relevant research, generate AI responses, maintain account security, and improve reliability.

AI processing and service providers

SciFit uses Supabase for authentication, database, private file storage, and server functions. When you use AI features, your prompt, relevant profile details, and selected image or video may be processed by Google Gemini to generate a response. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Research citations may link to third-party sites such as PubMed or Europe PMC. Their privacy practices apply after you leave SciFit.

Storage and retention

Some app data is stored locally in your browser. When you sign in, account data and uploads may also be stored in Supabase. We retain data while your account is active or as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

Your choices and deletion

You can choose not to provide optional information or upload media. You may clear locally stored data through your browser or device settings. To request access to or deletion of account data and uploaded files, email aryan.wagh05@gmail.com from the address associated with your account.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including authenticated access, per-user database rules, and private file storage. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid submitting information you do not want processed by the service.

Children's privacy

SciFit is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so it can be removed.

Health information

SciFit provides general educational fitness information and is not a medical service. Do not use the app as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SciFit changes. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to Aryan Wagh at aryan.wagh05@gmail.com.
